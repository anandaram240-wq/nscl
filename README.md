# MCE Hassan — 23CS702 Network Security and Cyber Law
## Interactive Question-First CIE & SEE Examination Preparation Platform

An advanced, interactive examination mastery platform built specifically for the **23CS702 Cryptography and Network Security** course at **Malnad College of Engineering (MCE), Hassan** (Autonomous Institution affiliated with VTU), Department of Computer Science & Engineering (7th Semester).

🌐 **Live Platform**: [https://anandaram240-wq.github.io/nscl/](https://anandaram240-wq.github.io/nscl/)

---

## 🎯 Key Features

1. **Intelligent Question Consolidation & OR-Based Grouping**:
   - 41 Historical Examination Questions (2018–2025) consolidated into **16 Comprehensive Master Question Groups**.
   - Preserves 100% of historical question variations with `─── OR ───` badges, paper codes, exam years, marks, and scanned paper references.
   - Frequency calculated using unique examination papers (e.g. RSA Numericals: 6/9 papers = 66.7%).

2. **In-Place 7-Section Study Layout**:
   - **SECTION A**: Consolidated Historical Questions & OR-based Variants with one-click PDF scan viewer.
   - **SECTION B**: Exact Original Classroom Slide Diagrams extracted from Prof. Tejonidhi M R's lecture slides with interactive Zoom In (+), Zoom Out (-), Reset, and Fullscreen modal.
   - **SECTION C**: Clean Interactive Vector SVG Flowcharts with color-coded stage highlights.
   - **SECTION D**: Exact Answer from MCE Classroom Notes (verbatim text and slide citations).
   - **SECTION E**: Simple Exam-Ready Master Answer (covering the UNION of all variant requirements).
   - **SECTION F**: Quick Memorization & Examinee Checklist (Key points, keywords, formulas, examiner traps).
   - **SECTION G**: Mathematical Step-by-Step Solvers for numerical problems (RSA with 6 variants, Knapsack inversion, Rabin 4-root CRT).

3. **Interactive Numerical Lab**:
   - Dedicated calculators for **RSA Cryptosystem**, **Merkle-Hellman Knapsack**, and **Rabin Cryptosystem**.
   - Step-by-step modular exponentiation arithmetic and Extended Euclidean algorithm solvers.

4. **Technical Diagrams Laboratory**:
   - Side-by-side vector diagrams and high-resolution classroom slides for AES round structure, data units, block-to-state mapping, asymmetric cryptography, and IT Act provisions.

5. **Mock CIE Simulator & Timed Test**:
   - 60-minute examination simulation generating balanced question sets across Module 3 (AES, RSA, Knapsack/Rabin) and Module 4 (IT Act 2000).
   - Rubric-based self-scoring system.

6. **Flashcards & 1-Page Pre-Exam Cram Sheet**:
   - High-yield flashcard deck for rapid recall of statutory section numbers, polynomial constants, and encryption formulas.
